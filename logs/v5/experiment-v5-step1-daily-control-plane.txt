V5 Step 1 daily control-plane evidence (NOT STRATEGY PERFORMANCE)

Implemented:
- daily BUY, SELL, HOLD, DEFER, and BLOCK decisions
- non-empty named satisfiers required for every target intent
- no order unless all satisfiers pass and target differs from current position
- open positions without a daily target defer rather than liquidate silently
- chronological, duplicate-resistant daily plans and metrics
- later-open-only, sell-before-buy, idempotent paper execution
- per-order deferral for missing opening evidence
- realistic cash-equity fees and adverse INR 0.05 slippage
- buy value, cash, maximum-position, kill-switch, and reconciliation controls
- sales are never blocked merely because an appreciated position exceeds buy limits
- exact-date closing marks, paper equity, fees, return, drawdown, NIFTY benchmark,
  and excess return
- atomic restart state and append-only daily journal
- live adapter remains disabled

Interpretation:
Step 1 proves only that the daily machinery can run and preserve decisions. It does
not prove a daily trading edge. V5 Step 2 must predeclare one fixed strategy policy
before development comparison or prospective shadow observation.
